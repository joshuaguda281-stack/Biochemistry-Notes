Biochemistry Notes

Biochemistry is the study of chemical processes within and relating to living organisms. It is a laboratory-based science that combines biology and chemistry. By using chemical knowledge and techniques, biochemists can understand and solve biological problems.

Key areas of study in biochemistry include:

    Fundamental Concepts:
  • Structure and function of biomolecules (proteins, nucleic acids, carbohydrates, lipids)
  • Enzyme function and kinetics
  • Metabolic pathways and regulation
  • Genetic information flow and expression
  • Cell signaling and communication
  • Structural biology and protein folding
  • Membrane biology and transport mechanisms
  • Biochemical techniques and instrumentation
  • Applications in medicine, agriculture, and biotechnology
  • Understanding diseases at the molecular level
